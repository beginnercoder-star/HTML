Privacy Policy

Orantes Painting LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request through our website, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service requested and service area
  • Project description
  • Photos you choose to upload of your project

We do not collect any information automatically beyond what you submit through our contact form.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your quote request
  • Contact you about scheduling an estimate or consultation
  • Provide the painting services you've requested

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our contact form is processed through Formspree, a third-party form service that securely delivers your submission to us by email. Formspree's own privacy practices can be found at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy (opens in a new tab)

Our service area map uses OpenStreetMap, which does not require any personal information from visitors to display.

Data Retention

We retain the information from your quote request only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and complete any resulting project.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request that we delete your information from our records.
